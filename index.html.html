New madrasa · Jesi, Italy

A madrasa where the children of Jesi learn Arabic and grow in knowledge

Muslim Center Jesi, run by Baitul Ummah APS, is being founded to give children a place to study and to learn Arabic. We are raising our founding fund, and every gift from anywhere in the world brings the first class closer.

    About us

    Why we are building this

    We believe children should have a place in Jesi where they can learn Arabic, read the Qur’an and study in a caring setting. Muslim Center Jesi is being founded to be that place.

    The centre is run by Baitul Ummah APS, an association in Italy. We will post news here as the work moves forward.
